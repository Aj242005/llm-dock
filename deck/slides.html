04 / 16
One dashboard. One closed loop.
Plan
A planner with six tools
A LangChain planner calls six inspection and submission tools. Vertex AI by default; OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter and Anthropic selectable. It measures, checks the gate, previews the manoeuvre, then submits — with a rationale.
Advise
A policy that suggests
An expert-initialized PPO advisor supplies a learned proposal — a 4-second lookahead closing vector. The planner may adopt it, cap it or reject it, and every decision records which, with a reason.
Fly
A controller that never guesses
An LQR controller tracks the accepted target continuously, under per-axis force, torque, acceleration and stopping-distance envelopes on a 500 kg chaser. Physics never waits for the planner — it keeps stepping while guidance is pending.
And underneath everything
An independent safety channel reads simulator truth, enforces the motion and capture gates, and performs fault braking — surviving provider loss and stale sensor data.

What this is not.
Stated up front, because the first serious question is: what does this actually prove?
Not flight-certified
Research software, with no certification of any kind.
Local linearised dynamics
The primary's motion is prescribed, not controlled.
No contact model
No contact mechanics, structural dynamics or plume interaction.
No sensor estimation
The safety channel reads simulator truth — not a flight-ready estimator.
Demonstrations, not rates
Provider runs are individual live checks, not a success rate.
Archived policy limits
Stored checkpoints were trained at a 1.25 m gate; the 2 cm environment is packaged for fresh training.
Process-local sessions
Four active and 32 retained sessions in memory, no authentication.
Fuel and geometry are assumed
Thruster efficiency, lever arm and inertias are editable simulator assumptions.

Roadmap
Docking Lab · investor brief
14 / 16
From simulator to qualification.
Three phases, proposed with a partner — none has started.
Phase 01
Partner dynamics
Plug a partner propagator and actuator model in, re-run the reference scenarios, and publish a paired comparison against the current stack.
Exit: the same capture gate passed on partner dynamics.
Phase 02
Hardware in the loop
Real flight-computer timing, sensor models and injected comms latency — then verify that stale plans are discarded and recovery stays bounded.
Exit: latency-bounded replanning demonstrated.
Phase 03
Qualification pack
Traceability from requirement to scenario to telemetry to hash, with failure cases retained and a reviewer-runnable bundle.
Exit: an independent reviewer reproduces a claim unaided.
What stays constant across all three phases: the gate that decides what counts as capture, the six tools the planner may call, and the evidence format a reviewer reads. The vehicle underneath them is yours.
